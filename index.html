These practice tests are modeled after the California Assessment of Student Performance and Progress (CAASPP) Smarter Balanced tests for English Language Arts and Math. Each test is timed, just like the real thing, and gives you a full score breakdown by topic when you finish.

You can take a test online (click Start Test) or print it on paper (click Print Blank Copy or Print Answer Key).
